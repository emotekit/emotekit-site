10-minute workouts

Choose three easy beginner exercises—push-ups, sit-ups, squats, planks, and more—and start your workout. Training alongside your favorite animal makes even tough sessions feel easier.

As your animals grow, they learn new poses and personal mottos. They will be there to keep you motivated!

Scout new animals

Work out three times to get a scouting chance. You might even meet a rare muscular animal...

These animals are quiet and disciplined, but they have plenty to say through their actions. Talk to them often for helpful advice and unexpected surprises.

Build your own
animal park

Choose a pose, background, and motto (or catchphrase) to create an exhibit of your own. You might even win a place in the weekly contest!

With the friend feature, you can share your daily exhibits, cheer each other on, and celebrate your progress together.

They might escape...?

Your animals' motivation (MP) drops by one every day. If it reaches zero, they may run away from the park...!

Work out every day to keep their motivation high. On days when you cannot train, give them protein to keep them going!
